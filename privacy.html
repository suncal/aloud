Aloud — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Aloud is a free communication (AAC) app that lets a person tap or type and have their phone speak for them. We designed it to be private by default. This policy explains, plainly, what data the app handles and what it does not.

The short version: No account. No ads. No analytics. No tracking. We do not sell or share your data. Everything you say stays on your device — unless you deliberately turn on the optional “Care circle,” explained below.

Information stored on your device only

The following are saved only in your device's local storage and are never sent to us: your typed and tapped messages, your custom phrases, your settings (voice, speed, text size, switch-scanning, big buttons), your patient/family names and phone number, and your on-device care log (a history of what was said). You can clear these at any time from within the app or by clearing your browser/app data.

Text-to-speech

Speech is produced by your device's own built-in text-to-speech voices. The text you speak is processed by your operating system to generate audio; Aloud does not send it to us.

Optional “Care circle” (off by default)

If you choose to turn on Sync to care circle, the messages you speak are sent to the third-party service ntfy.sh under a care-circle code, so that family members you invite can view them live and receive urgent alerts. Please understand:

  • This data leaves your device and is transmitted to, and briefly stored by, ntfy.sh (typically for a short retention window of around 12 hours).
  • Access is controlled only by the care-circle code — anyone who has the code could view the messages. Treat the code as a secret and share it only with people you trust.
  • This feature is off unless you enable it, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • ntfy.sh has its own privacy terms; see their website.

Because this can carry sensitive, health-related information, we recommend enabling Sync only when you understand the above. A future version will add authenticated, encrypted storage for stronger privacy.

Alerts and summaries you send to family

The “Alert family” and “Send summary” features open your phone's own messaging (SMS/share) with a pre-filled message. These are sent by you, through your phone, to the recipient you choose. Aloud does not process, store, or receive a copy of them.

Children and vulnerable users

Aloud is intended as an assistive tool and may be used by or on behalf of children or adults who need communication support. Caregivers should review the Care-circle section above before enabling any sharing feature.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, microphone or camera data. Aloud requires no microphone or camera permission to function.
